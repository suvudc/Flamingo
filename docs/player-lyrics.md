# Player gestures and lyrics (Music build 26)

The album player uses Flamingo's original 0.56 artwork/title allocation and keeps the existing square artwork, animated-cover service, controls and minimize artwork flight. A vertical gesture is observed in the initial pointer pass so child click targets cannot trap a swipe from the artwork or middle of the player. Movement is measured in root coordinates so moving the sheet cannot cancel its own drag distance. A deliberate 64dp downward swipe dismisses without requiring a half-screen pull. Direction locking leaves horizontal seeking and volume adjustment with their sliders. Lyrics and queue lists retain their own scrolling.

The main player's lyric strip sits above the seek bar and opens the full lyrics page. Both views use the same timeline model and rendering approach: source word boundaries drive a feathered sweep, the full view lifts a singing word by up to 2dp, lines hand over over 400ms, and the strip slides over 340ms. Motion values and visual behavior are adapted from `suvudc/BitChord`'s GPL-3.0 `sharedUi/.../PlayerLyrics.kt`; Music's renderer is written against its existing parsed-lyrics model and Compose baseline. Line-only lyrics highlight by line. No word timestamps are fabricated. Instrumental gaps do not keep displaying the previous line in the strip. The draw clock is active only while the player view is mounted and the app is foregrounded; inactive lines do not read it during drawing.

“Lyrics provider” is available from the player overflow menu and from the full lyrics page's source/status header. Choices are Automatic, Local lyrics, lrc.red, BiniLyrics, BetterLyrics and LRCLIB. Selection persists. Automatic prefers local word lyrics and otherwise checks online sources for genuine word timings. A chosen online source does not silently change to a different source. “Download / refresh lyrics” retries that selection. Successful responses are saved under filesDir/downloaded-lyrics, separately for each provider. Automatic can also reuse any already-downloaded provider version offline. Existing automatic downloads retain their original keys. Changing source cancels the previous display job, preventing a stale response from replacing the new selection.

Validation covers word gaps, seeking backwards, line-only data, single-word TTML, missing/empty lines, provider cache isolation, and gesture direction locking. The Android runtime script opens the overflow menu and provider picker, then checks that a swipe from the middle of the artwork returns to the navigation tabs. A synthetic 64-second WAV and original TTML fixture exercise real timed text drawing, and a short screen recording captures the full lyric animation. It dismisses a system ANR dialog only when it explicitly belongs to the emulator's Pixel Launcher; an app ANR is never ignored.
